Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

Grindly is built to help you lock in — privately. In short: everything core runs on your device, we don't require an account, your Screen Time selections are anonymous tokens we literally cannot read, and we don't sell your data.

Data stored on your device

Your grind tasks, block profiles, lock-in sessions, day records, and streak history are stored locally on your device. We do not upload or back up this data to our servers.

Screen Time selections are opaque

When you choose apps or categories to block, iOS (via Apple's Family Controls framework) hands Grindly opaque tokens — not names. Grindly cannot see which specific apps you selected. The blocking itself is performed on-device by Apple's Screen Time system; no selection data leaves your device.

Optional Anti-Cheat photo check-in (Pro)

If you turn on photo verification for a task (a premium feature), the photo is sent to our secure proxy for a single yes/no verdict on whether the task looks done, then it is immediately discarded. It is never stored by us and never used to train anything. This feature is off by default and capped by a hard monthly quota.

Optional AI discipline coach (Pro, text only)

The optional AI coach sends only text — your streak length and completion rate — to our proxy (which calls Google's Gemini API) to return a short motivational nudge. No images are sent, and there is no image generation anywhere in Grindly. This feature is premium and hard-capped each month.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple provides anonymous transaction status to unlock premium features.

Analytics

Grindly may record anonymous, aggregate product events (for example, that a lock-in session was started) to improve the app. These events are not tied to your identity and contain no personal content.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not read which apps you block.
  • We do not require a social account or track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not generate images.

Your choices

You can export or permanently delete all of your data at any time from Settings in the app.

Children

Grindly is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
